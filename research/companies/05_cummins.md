## 2. Products and specifications

### 2a. On-highway (global)
- **X15 (2027 GHG27 / HELM):** connectivity built in, via OEM integration or Cummins' own **Acumen** module. Source: [Fleet Equipment](https://www.fleetequipmentmag.com/?p=179692). Date: 2025.
- **Cummins Connected Solutions for 2027:** diesel, natural-gas and (later) **hydrogen** engines; "Predictive Insights and Maintenance", "Remote Diagnostics". Source: [Cummins news, 7 May 2025](https://www.cummins.com/news/2025/05/07/getting-more-less-2027-cummins-connected-solutions). Date: **7 May 2025**.
- **Predictability system:** Cummins unveiled a predictability system for its engines. Source: [Heavy Equipment Magazine](https://www.heavyquipmag.com/2025/04/22/cummins-unveils-a-predictability-system-for-its-engines/). Date: **22 Apr 2025**.
- **X15H hydrogen ICE (concept, 2022–23):** 15 L; swap-in for diesel of similar displacement; up to **1,900 lb-ft** torque; conversion claim >500 miles range. Production year not given (2022 report pointed to ~2027). Source: [CCJ](https://www.ccjdigital.com/alternative-power/article/15292318/cummins-inc-cummins-hydrogen-engine-offers-advantages-over-allelectric). Date: 2022–23.
- **Platform Science partnership (TMC25):** "Vehicle Health Intelligence" integration offering remote diagnostics and engine-data insights to fleets. Source: [Truck Parts & Service](https://www.truckpartsandservice.com/technology/telematics/article/15739547/platform-science-cummins-announce-partnership-at-tmc). Date: 2025.

### 2b. Off-highway / mining
- **PrevenTech:** real-time remote monitoring, predictive analytics, 24/7 expert support. Updated for bauma 2025. Source: [Canadian Mining Journal](https://canadianminingjournal.com/news/cummins-unveils-enhanced-preventech-platform-at-bauma-2025). Date: 2025.
- **Company-reported result (2024, two mine sites, 340 engines):** average **45+ hours** downtime prevented and **$70,000** maintenance cost avoided per engine. Source: same. **Company claim.**
- **AI on QSK60 trucks (2025 case study):** AI on **80 million data points** from **50 trucks**. Source: [3BL Media / CSRwire](https://www.3blmedia.com/news/evolving-technologies-how-advanced-digital-solutions-are-powering-safety-and-productivity). **Company-sponsored.**

### 2c. India products (BS VI and gensets)
- **BS VI SCR engines** for Indian CVs; company says SCR can cut NOx by up to **99%**. Source: [Cummins India BSVI](https://www.cummins.com/en/in/engines/bsvi-engines). Company claim.
- **Single Module ATS and No-EGR** compliant engines; **L10** engine rated **360–460 hp**; hydrogen **B6.7H**. Listed on Cummins' EXCON 2025 page. Source: [Cummins EXCON 2025](https://www.cummins.com/events/2025/excon?trk=test), [Cummins India BSVI](https://www.cummins.com/en/in/engines/bsvi-engines).
- **Genset:** **CPCB IV+ compliant 82.5 kVA** launched at EXCON 2025 (Jan 2026 coverage). Source: [Powerline](https://powerline.net.in/2026/01/13/cummins-india-launches-cpcb-iv-compliant-82-5-kva-genset-at-excon-2025/). Date: Jan 2026.
- **Fuel-agnostic platform and Single Module ATS** introduced at **SIAT 2024**. Source: [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/cummins-introduces-fuel-agnostic-platform-and-single-module-aftertreatment-system-at-siat-2024-118853). Date: 2024.
- **New engine platform and hydrogen fuel system in India** (trade report). Source: [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/cummins-unveils-new-engine-platform-and-hydrogen-fuel-system-in-india-124461?amp=1). Date: 2025 (n/d exact).

## 3. Engine and after-treatment technology

- **After-treatment:** SCR across BS VI; **Single Module ATS** (fuel-agnostic). Source: §2c.
- **No EGR** in some BS VI engines (Single Module ATS). Source: EXCON 2025 page.
- **Hydrogen ICE:** X15H concept; B6.7H (India); TCPL GES production at Jamshedpur. Sources: §2a, §1.
- **AdBlue/AUS 32 tuning:** Cummins India says its after-treatment can be tuned to cut AdBlue or diesel for thermal regen. Source: [Autocar Pro, Cummins India BS VI](https://www.autocarpro.in/feature/cummins-india-bets-on-bs-vi-to-showcase-technology-prowess-expand-business-56373). Date: 2020.
- **DPF:** Not described in the sources I could access. **Specific public DPF telemetry not found.**
