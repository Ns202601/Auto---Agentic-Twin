## 2. Products and specifications

### 2a. Diesel HCV/MHCV and engines
- **EXCON 2025 showcase:** **A6 380 hp CRS** heavy-duty engine; **H6 hydrogen / flex-fuel** engine; **H6 133 hp CEV Stage V** engine (no intercooler); **Compact Flex EATS** (flex after-treatment); next-gen **P15** and **H4 Unipack** series; 82.5 kVA diesel genset. Source: [Autocar Pro, Engines of Change](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/engines-of-change-ashok-leylands-diversification-gambit-pays-off-130400). Date: 2025.
- **BS6 / SCR details:** **not found** in this research. Confirm in the company's technical brochures.

### 2b. Electric (through **Switch Mobility**)
- **FY26 Switch India:** **1,530 e-buses** (+238% y/y), electric LCVs ~**1,600** units (+56%). Source: search summary of 2026 trade coverage (primary not opened; the [Autocar Pro FY26 expectation piece](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/ashok-leyland-expects-switch-india-to-report-net-profit-3x-bus-volume-growth-in-fy26-125608?amp=1) only gives the earlier expectation). Date: FY26 (reported 2026).
- **Maiden net profit (Switch Mobility), H1 FY26.** Production moved from UK to Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. Source: [Autocar Pro, Switch profit](https://www.91trucks.com/news/ashok-leyland-powers-ahead-in-ev-market-as-switch-mobility-hits-profit). Date: 2025–26.
- **Battery pack:** Ashok Leyland plans **in-house EV battery pack production by H1 FY28**, near Chennai. Source: [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/ashok-leyland-to-begin-in-house-ev-battery-pack-production-by-fy28-132820). Date: 2026 (announced).
- **Range of battery-electric trucks:** 2–3.5 t Switch models up to the **BOSS** electric truck (company claim). Source: [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/ashok-leyland-accelerates-multi-fuel-strategy-bets-on-evs-and-lng-ahead-of-hydrogen-adoption-126576). Date: 2025.
- **Electric freight corridor (Gujarat, 160 km, 55 t trucks):** reported June 2026. **Single secondary source; verify.**
- **Plant:** Switch e-bus plant in **Lucknow** inaugurated (trade report).
- **Mauritius:** 100 e-buses delivered (trade report). Source: [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/stocks/ashok-leyland-s-switch-mobility-delivers-100-electric-buses-to-mauritius).

### 2c. Hydrogen and LNG
- **H2-ICE truck:** India's first hydrogen-ICE truck unveiled in **2023**, developed with **Reliance Industries**. Source: [Hinduja Group newsletter, June 2025](https://hindujagroup.com/newsletter/june2025/al-ashok-leyland-hydrogen-powered-vehicles.html).
- **Management view:** EV and LNG will arrive before hydrogen. Source: [Autocar Pro, multi-fuel strategy](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/ashok-leyland-accelerates-multi-fuel-strategy-bets-on-evs-and-lng-ahead-of-hydrogen-adoption-126576).
- **LNG truck:** advanced development; rollout signalled for FY26. Trade report.
- **No 2026 hydrogen truck launch or sales** was found.

### 4a. Syncron partnership (predictive maintenance)
- Ashok Leyland partnered with **Syncron** (Swedish aftermarket SaaS) to add **failure-detection** to its **Condition Monitoring Systems**. Plan: scale vehicles equipped with "Uptime" and add **ML/AI prognostic recommendations** over three years. Sources: [Syncron](https://www.syncron.com/blog/ashok-leyland-in-partnership-with-syncron-to-propel-industrial-iot-transformation-for-predictive-vehicle-maintenance), [S&P Global Aftermarket Insight](https://aftermarketinsight.spglobal.com/news/607/swedish-aftermarket-saas-company-syncron-partners-with-indian-cv-maker-ashok-leyland-). **Date not visible** (likely 2022–23). Treat as **announced**; the ML outcome is not published.

### 4b. Company-reported results (single source family, not independently verified)
- "Double-digit gains in vehicle uptime" and "40% reduction in heavy-duty collisions" (July 2026 report). Verify on the company's own statement.
