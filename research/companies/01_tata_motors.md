## 2. Products and vehicle specifications

### 2a. Electric (confirmed product launches)
- **17 next-generation trucks, 7–55 t**, including electric, launched on the **I-MOEV (Intelligent Modular Electric Vehicle) architecture** under the Tata Trucks.ev brand. Lineup named in coverage: Ultra EV (7, 9, 12 t), Prima E.55S electric prime mover, Prima E.28K electric tipper.
  - Sources: [Rushlane](https://www.rushlane.com/tata-motors-launches-17-new-trucks-expands-electric-portfolio-12538413.html), [Tata World (company news)](https://www.tataworld.com/news/openinside/tata-motors-launches-17-next-gen-trucks), [EPC World](https://www.epcworld.in/tata-motors-launches-next-generation-truck-portfolio-with-azura-series-and-tata-trucks-ev/). Date: **Jan 2026**.
  - Battery pack range: 90–450 kWh modular packs per one source; 96–450 kWh per another. **Sources conflict.**
- **Prima E.55S:** 470 kW, 450 kWh pack (one source says 453 kWh), **up to 350 km range** per Tata's own material.
  - First deliveries to **BillionE Mobility on 10 Apr 2026**; order for 250 more units reported.
  - Sources: [Tata World](https://www.tataworld.com/news/openinside/tata-motors-electric-prima-e55s-billione-mobility-zero-emission-freight), [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/tata-motors-delivers-first-electric-prima-e55s-trucks-to-billione-mobility-132067). Date: Apr 2026.
- **EV volumes:** "rose 59% vs 2024-25" (chairman's FY26 letter). Source: [Chairman AGM speech](https://www.motorindiaonline.in/driving-the-next-chapter-of-commercial-mobility/). Date: 2026.
- **Unverified:** a "250+ km" range for "Tata Trucks.ev" (one summary; model not identified).

### 2b. Hydrogen (trials and MoUs, not series production)
- **Hydrogen trials** flagged off **4 Mar 2025** in New Delhi. **16 hydrogen trucks**, both **H2-ICE and fuel-cell (FCEV)**, including Prima H.55S and Prima H.28. Trial runs up to 24 months. Funded under the **National Green Hydrogen Mission** (MNRE tender).
  - Sources: [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/tata-motors-begins-hydrogen-truck-trials-in-india-125235), [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/tata-motors-begins-trials-of-hydrogen-powered-heavy-duty-trucks-for-long-haul-transportation).
- **Port MoU:** 40 green-hydrogen heavy trucks with V.O. Chidambaranar Port Authority (Tuticorin). Plan: hydrogen prime mover trial first, then H2-ICE phased over ~2 years. Source: [Tata World](https://www.tataworld.com/news/openinside/tata-motors-voc-port-green-hydrogen-trucks-tuticorin-2026). Date: 2026.
- **Hydrogen ICE engine production:** Cummins-built H2-ICE engines for Tata trucks at a Jamshedpur facility, via Tata Cummins' GES unit (see §3). Source: [Fleet.ie](https://fleet.ie/cummins-produces-hydrogen-internal-combustion-engines-for-tata-motors-at-new-manufacturing-facility/). Date: n/d (2025-era).
- **Management stance (quoted in coverage):** battery electric is not seen as viable for heavy long-haul, so hydrogen runs alongside batteries. Source: [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/tata-motors-begins-trials-of-hydrogen-powered-heavy-duty-trucks-for-long-haul-transportation).

### 2c. Diesel / ICE (BS6 range)
- **BS6 range launch, Aug 2020 (primary, Tata press release):** Optimised drivetrains with **SCR** and **EGR+SCR** after-treatment, Gear Shift Advisor, multi-mode fuel-economy switch, fuel-economy gains "up to 12%" on select models. Source: [Tata Motors press release, 27 Aug 2020](https://www.tatamotors.com/?p=27012). Status: **primary (press release), but 2020 and a claim by the company.**
- **BS6 3.8 L NA SGI CNG engine:** ARAI BS6 certification announced **18 Jan 2019** (first OEM in India to get BS6 for NA CNG engine for CVs, per company). After-treatment is a **three-way catalytic converter**, not SCR/DPF. Source: [MarketScreener, 18 Jan 2019](https://in.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/TATA-MOTORS-LIMITED-9058835/news/Tata-Motors-receives-BS6-compliance-certification-for-Tata-3-8L-NA-SGI-CNG-engine-27883703/).
- **Lubricant:** 5W30 synthetic oil for BS6 diesel engines, launched 2022. Source: [Autocar Pro](https://autocarpro.in/news-national/tata-motors-launches-5w30-synthetic-engine-oil-for-cvs-102841).
- **DPF:** I found **no Tata-specific DPF release** in this research. Not claimed here.

## 3. Engine and after-treatment partner: Tata Cummins

- **Tata Cummins Private Limited (TCPL)** is a **50:50 JV** of Tata Motors and Cummins Inc. Sales are restricted to the two parent groups under the JV agreement. Source: [ICRA rating report](https://www.icra.in/Rating/GetRationalReportFilePdf?id=140553) (2025).
- **TCPL GES** (Green Energy Solutions), set up **March 2023**, is a wholly owned subsidiary for low- and zero-emission propulsion. Listed products: after-treatment systems, turbochargers, fuel systems. Source: [CSR Company profile](https://www.csr-company.com/node/3760); ICRA (above).
- **SCR:** Tata's 2017 BS-IV announcement described urea-based SCR (AdBlue), co-developed with Tata Cummins. Source: [Autocar Pro, BS-IV](https://www.autocarpro.in/news-national/tata-motors-ready-bs-iv-cvs-egr-scr-tech-24476).
- **Engine-health monitoring at the engine level:** not publicly described in the sources I could access. Cummins' own engine-health tooling is covered in its report (see `05_cummins.md`).

### 4a. Fuel-efficiency AI: **Mileage Sarathi**
- Fleet AI tool for fuel-efficiency. Company: **median 6–7%** fuel-saving across tested fleets; mileage guarantee with compensation if the target is missed.
- Tata's campaign page: **5.5%–6.3%** median improvement. A third-party analysis cited **4.8%** across ~340,000 trucks. **The figures differ; the sources do not explain why.**
- Identifies harsh acceleration, poor gear selection and idling (per coverage).
- Sources: [Mileage Sarathi (Tata CV blog)](https://cv.tatamotors.com/blogs/meet-mileage-saarathi-tata-motors-smartest-fuel-efficiency-guide-for-your-fleet); [Autocar Pro, digital arm](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/tata-motors-digital-arm-shifts-from-products-to-platform-play-133191?amp=1); [TrucksBuses](https://www.trucksbuses.com/news/tata-motors-bets-on-data-to-shape-the-future-of-commercial-vehicles).

### 4b. Revenue model (company stated, Jun 2026)
Four planned layers: platform subscription, transaction take-rate, value-added services, data/software-defined-vehicle unlocks. Target ~3 million connected vehicles in five years. Source: [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/tata-motors-digital-arm-shifts-from-products-to-platform-play-133191).

## 5. Engine health, digital twins, AI and computing

- **Engine-health monitoring:** Fleet Edge "vehicle health" alerts. Specific sensor list (coolant, oil pressure, DPF, etc.) **not found in public sources.**
- **Digital twin:** Tata Motors and Jaguar Land Rover use **NVIDIA Omniverse** to build digital twins of manufacturing facilities and simulate vehicle development. **Note:** this is the Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles / JLR side, not the CV unit. Source: [Autocar Pro, AI adoption](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/tata-motors-deepens-ai-adoption-across-product-development-manufacturing-and-service-133239?amp=1). Date: 2026.
- **Aerodynamic simulation:** ~1,500 runs per day vs ~50 earlier. Same source. Passenger vehicles side.
- **AI in manufacturing and service:** vision inspection, condition-based equipment monitoring, spare-parts forecasting, service advisory, damage assessment (TMPV). Source: same as above.
- **Platform:** t.idal, an in-house SDV/digital backbone for future products. Source: [Whalesbook](https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/technology/Tata-Motors-Bets-on-AI-Driven-tidal-Platform-for-Future-EVs/6a38e794a1c5bf084328959e). Date: 2026. Passenger vehicle focus.
- **Data scale and access:** 12 PB claim (see §4). Telemetry the OEM streams is governed by the OEM's data agreement with the fleet.
