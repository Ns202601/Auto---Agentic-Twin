## 2. Products and specifications

### 2a. BS6 truck and bus range — **EUTECH6**
- Covers **4.9 to 55 t**. Combines Volvo Euro VI engineering with Eicher drive lines. Source: [Autocar Pro, BS VI range](https://www.autocarpro.in/news-national/eicher-s-new-bs-vi-truck-and-bus-range-promises-reduced-fuel-costs-improved-productivity-55748). Date: **Mar 2020** (BS VI national launch); unveiled June 2019.
- Company test claim: exhaust after-treatment and engine tested for **>5.6 million km**. Same source.

### 2b. Eicher Pro X (small commercial, electric and CNG/diesel)
- **Launched 17 Jan 2025** at Bharat Mobility Global Expo, per [Eicher press release](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/eicher-trucks-and-buses-launches-eicher-pro-x-range-at-bharat-mobility-global-expo-2025-124425?amp=1). EV first; CNG and diesel to follow.
- **EV battery packs:** **32 kWh and 40 kWh**. Sources: [wagenclub](https://wagenclub.com/2025/01/eicher-pro-x-electric-mini-trucks-scv-range-launched-price-details.html).
- **Certified range:** Eicher's small-trucks site cites **206–249 km** by pack and configuration. A 2026 review cites ~249 km (40 kWh) and 206–220 km (32 kWh). **Use Eicher's official figure, and note the 206 vs 220 km gap.** Source: [smalltrucks.eichertrucksandbuses.com](https://smalltrucks.eichertrucksandbuses.com/newsroom/press-release/eicher-trucks-and-buses-launches-eicher-pro-x-range-at-bharat-mobility-global-expo-2025).
- **GVW / payload:** 3.5 t GVW; payload about 1,700 kg (listing); one 2026 review says ~1,760 kg for the EV variant. **Verify.**
- **Price:** ~₹16.99–17.99 lakh ex-showroom (one 2026 blog; **unverified**).
- **Production and deliveries:** Production moved to VECV's **Bhopal** plant; deliveries from **Feb 2025**. Source: [Eicher, VECV Bhopal facility](https://smalltrucks.eichertrucksandbuses.com/newsroom/press-release/vecv-inaugurates-manufacturing-facility-and-starts-deliveries-of-eicher-pro-x-range).
- **Fleet example:** Eicher and Magenta Mobility launched **100 Pro X EV** units. Source: [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/eicher-trucks-and-magenta-mobility-launch-100-eicher-pro-x-electric-trucks-125463?amp=1).

## 3. Engine and emission technology

- **Engine origin:** BS VI engines built on **Volvo Euro VI base engines**. VECV says it has supplied Euro VI base engines to Volvo Group for over six years, with **>120,000 units** at that point (company profile). Sources: [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/feature/vecv-revs-up-to-drive-home-its-firstmover-advantage-in-bs-vi-era-55734?amp=1), [Motown India, VEPT export](https://motownindia.com/Bureau/Auto-Industry/1888/VECV-to-export-Euro-6-engine-base-from-Pithampur).
- **VEPT (Pithampur):** Euro VI engine production since **2013**; Phase 1 capacity **25,000 units/yr** (at launch); current ~**75,000** units of 5 L and 8 L engines per trade coverage. Serves Volvo Group globally, with Euro 6 base engines going to Volvo's Vénissieux plant, France. Sources: [Autocar Pro, VEPT](https://www.autocarpro.in/feature/vecv-revs-up-to-drive-home-its-firstmover-advantage-in-bs-vi-era-55734?amp=1), [Motown India](https://motownindia.com/Bureau/Auto-Industry/1888/VECV-to-export-Euro-6-engine-base-from-Pithampur). Capacity figures are from different years, so check them.
- **After-treatment:** BS VI uses **SCR** ("duty-cycle based SCR for high reliability and low maintenance"). Public text **does not name DPF hardware**. Source: [Autocar Pro, BS VI range](https://www.autocarpro.in/news-national/eicher-s-new-bs-vi-truck-and-bus-range-promises-reduced-fuel-costs-improved-productivity-55748).
- **Engine plant process:** one trade report says assembly is vertical and the leak test happens once the injector is fitted. Single source; low confidence.
- **Not found:** VECV hydrogen engine work, DPF regeneration data, NOx sensor data, DEF public data.

### 4a. **Uptime NXT** (AI service layer, launched Oct 2026)
- Digital service ecosystem to reduce downtime, improve fleet performance and control operating costs. Built on the 2019 Uptime Centre.
- **Uptime Control Tower:** monitors service delivery and operations.
- **Fleet services:** fuel advisory, driver scorecards, operation insights, immobiliser support, proactive interventions. Source: [Construction World](https://www.constructionworld.in/latest-construction-technology/eicher-launches-ai-powered-uptime-nxt-fleet-services/98739).
- **Service One:** **generative-AI** tool for mechanics and technicians; supports diagnosis and guided repair steps; aimed at smaller operators with vernacular diagnostics. Source: [Autocar Pro, Uptime NXT launch](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/eicher-trucks-buses-launches-ai-powered-uptime-nxt-services-135202) and [Autocar Pro feature, de-skilling diagnostics](https://www.autocarpro.in/feature/eicher-bets-de-skilling-diagnostics-will-hook-indias-small-truck-fleets-135241).
- **Launch date:** **13 Oct 2026** (CMV360, Autocar Pro).
- **Status:** Announced service. **Accuracy of any predictive model is not published.**
