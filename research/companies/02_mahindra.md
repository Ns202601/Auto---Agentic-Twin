## 2. Products and specifications

### 2a. Diesel HCV (BLAZO range)
- **BLAZO X (BS6)**: 7.2 L mPOWER/FuelSmart engine, **206 kW / 1050 Nm** (per Mahindra brochure in search snippet). Multi-axle rigid, tipper, cargo and tractor-trailer, 28–55 t, **CIRT BS6 homologation** (multi-axle). Sources: [Autocar Pro, BS VI certification](https://www.autocarpro.in/news-national/mahindra-blazo-x-range-gets-bs-vi-certification-from-cirt-45351?amp=1), [Mahindra Blazo X transit mixer brochure](https://www.mahindratruckandbus.com/english/pdf/Blazo-X-Transit-Mixer-Brochure.pdf).
- **BLAZO i-TRK (launched 14 Aug 2026):** 7.2 L mPOWER, reported **320 hp / 1,100 Nm**, claimed **up to 10% better mileage** (vs previous models; baseline not stated), **48-hour uptime guarantee with ₹10,000/day payout** (subject to T&Cs). Sources: [Mahindra press release](https://www.mahindra.com/news-room/press-release/en/mahindra-launches-blazo-i-trk-revolutionizing-profitability-for-indian-truck-owners), [Autocar Pro, 320 hp](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/mahindra-blazo-i-trk-launched-with-320hp-engine-48-hour-uptime-134098), [Equipment India](https://equipmentindia.com/construction-equipment-updates/product-updates/Mahindra-Unveils-BLAZO-i-TRK-With-10-Better-Mileage/130802).
- **Mileage guarantee:** Mahindra launched a mileage guarantee for its BS6 OBD-II range in **July 2024**. Source: [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/mahindra-launches-mileage-guarantee-for-its-range-of-bs6-obd-ii-trucks-121387).

### 2b. Electric
- **e-ZEO (light electric SCV):** One source gives a **21.3 kWh** liquid-cooled battery and **160 km** range. Another (2025) gives **up to 120 km**. **Sources conflict.** Source: [91trucks e-ZEO](https://91trucks.com/trucks/mahindra/e-zeo/base).
- **Electric heavy trucks:** Early development. Mahindra's head of the segment argued a 500 km electric truck would need about **2 t of battery**, cutting payload, so short-haul is the first target. Source: [TrucksBuses, Mahindra begins work on electric trucks](https://www.trucksbuses.com/blog/mahindra-begins-work-on-electric-trucks-for-india). No Blazo-branded EV launch was found.
- **Electric bus:** SML Mahindra targets an electric bus launch by **Q4 FY27 or early next fiscal**, aimed at staff and school transport. Source: [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/sml-mahindra-expects-margin-recovery-by-q4fy27-plans-electric-bus-launch).
- **Policy:** PM E-DRIVE portal (Ministry of Heavy Industries) reported to provide ₹500 cr for up to 5,643 electric trucks. Source: search summary of [Autocar Pro, Mahindra electric trucks FY27](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/mahindra-starts-work-on-electric-trucks-first-ev-bus-due-in-fy27-134116?amp=1); **the PM E-DRIVE portal itself was not opened, so verify on the official MHI page.**

### 2c. Other
- Furio (ICV/LCV), Cruzio (bus) carry the BS6 OBD-II range and iMAXX (per 2020 launch coverage). Source: [TrucksBuses, BS6 range](https://www.trucksbuses.com/blog/mahindra-truck-and-bus-bs6-range-launched-auto-expo-2020/).

## 3. Engine and emission technology

- **BS6 approach:** Mahindra kept the engine largely unchanged. The main change was the **after-treatment system**, from BS-IV's simple SCR to **SCR + DOC + DPF + EGR** on the CRDe engine family. Source: [Mahindra BS6 coverage, Motorindia](https://www.motorindiaonline.in/mahindra-truck-bus-braces-for-new-emission-standards/).
- **Design position:** Mahindra's engineering head argued **SCR** suits HCVs better than EGR, which can shorten engine life. Mahindra also described an **airless SCR** design. Source: same Motorindia link.
- **AdBlue:** Mahindra markets low AdBlue consumption as a selling point. Source: [Mahindra BS6 product material, via search](https://www.mahindratruckandbus.com/pdf/hcv/blazo-brochure.pdf).
- **CNG:** CNG is offered on lighter Blazo models and the Jayo (4.8 t). **No CNG engine or after-treatment detail was found for the Blazo.**
- **Engine-health sensing:** Covered under telematics (§4).
- **Not found:** DPF regeneration logs, NOx sensor data, DEF consumption public data.

### 4a. On-vehicle AI (BLAZO i-TRK)
- The truck's power mode is chosen by an **in-vehicle chip**; AI computation runs **on the vehicle** rather than in the cloud. The telematics layer still connects out. Source: [Autocar Pro, AI on board](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/mahindra-puts-ai-on-board-its-new-heavy-truck-and-keeps-it-off-the-cloud-134104). Date: Aug 2026. This is one of the more specific architecture claims in this set; verify it on the press release.

## 5. Digital twins, AI and manufacturing

- **Group manufacturing AI ("MATRIX"):** includes predictive maintenance across plants. **Plant-level, not vehicle-level.** Source: [M&M IAR 2025-26](https://www.mahindra.com/annual-report-FY2026/45/).
- **Uptime.AI:** in-house agentic CMMS for plant uptime. Source: same IAR.
- **Factory digital twins:** "Smart Sequencing Digital Twin" for production planning; digital twin for a pre-docking conveyor. Plant-level. Source: same IAR.
- **Fleet/vehicle digital twin:** the 2020 iMAXX blog mentions a digital-twin platform. **No public product detail found.**
- **Group AI strategy:** deploy, transform, invent pillars (press coverage of FY26).
